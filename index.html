About This Project

CarbonLedger

CarbonLedger converts everyday activities — a commute, a meal, an hour of electricity — into kilograms of CO₂ equivalent using fixed emission factors, then surfaces the result through a live dashboard, a weekly target system, and a what-if simulator that models the impact of habit changes on real logged data.

🧱HTML5Semantic structure
🎨CSS3Custom properties, responsive grid
⚙️JavaScriptVanilla, no framework
📊Chart.jsData visualisation
💾localStorageOffline persistence
🌗ThemingDark & light modes

Feature Highlights

What's inside
  • Activity logging with live CO₂ preview and one-tap quick adds
  • Six fixed emission factors across travel, home and food
  • Dashboard with category breakdown, 7-day trend and emission calendar
  • Weekly target with pace tracking and exceeded-target alerts
  • History filterable by type, category, date range and free text
  • Eco Score grading consistency, target adherence and activity mix
  • What-if simulator modelling habit changes on your real data
  • Achievements, streaks, offset calculator and CSV export
